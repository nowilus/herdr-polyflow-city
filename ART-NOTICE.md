## How they were made

The characters, buildings, icons, map, sidebar and office tower were generated with OpenAI's
image generation, using PolyFlow's own artwork (its hero character, city panorama and palette)
as references. They were then keyed, cut, scaled with nearest-neighbour and assembled into GIFs
with ImageMagick. Many of the PNGs keep the generator's signed C2PA provenance record.
The rights in AI-generated images are not settled in every jurisdiction; use them at your own risk.

The netrunner is the PolyFlow hero. The other characters, the buildings, the map and the tower are
original designs and aren't based on a named game, film or character.

The preview images are screenshots of the layout in its demo mode: the agents shown are made up.
